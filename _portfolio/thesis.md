My M.Pharm thesis tested whether empagliflozin, an SGLT2 inhibitor, combined with probiotics could protect against memory loss in a high fat diet and choline induced model of Alzheimer's disease in BALB/c mice.

* Behavioural tests for learning and memory
* Biochemical markers of oxidative stress and inflammation
* 16S rRNA sequencing to profile changes in the gut microbiota
* TMAO, a gut derived metabolite, measured by LC-MS/MS
